Activity 2 · page 25

Past simple: yesterday and last year

Remember: We use the past simple for finished actions. go → went (irregular); cook → cooked (regular).
Example: Last year, we went on vacation.

Complete the six gaps with the past simple. Check them one by one.

wentwascamecookedtriedstayed
Activity 3 · page 25

Regular or irregular?

Write the past form and R or I in your notebook. Reveal one row at a time.

didsawhadateplayedIIIIR
Activity 4 · page 25

Build the questions

Reorder the words. Say the questions aloud before revealing them.

Book Activity 10 · writing

Write a short email together

With the teacher, make one short email: greeting, where you went, what you did, how you felt, and a closing.

Hi Sara,Last summer, I went to Ajman.We stayed at a resort and swam.It was wonderful!Your friend, Mariam

Students then write their own short email in their notebooks.

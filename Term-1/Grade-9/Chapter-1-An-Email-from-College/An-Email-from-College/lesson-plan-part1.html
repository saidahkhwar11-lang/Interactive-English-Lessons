1. Vocabulary + Audio 1 (8 mins) - Page 14
Strategy: Listen-Classify-Check
Teacher Role: Introduce word classes, play Audio 1, model pronunciation, pause for sorting and ask concept-check questions.
Student Role: Listen, repeat, classify nouns/prepositions/verbs/adjectives, compare with a partner and correct.
AFL: Listening observation, targeted questioning, partner check and progressive answer reveal.
2. Build the Email Game (8 mins)
Strategy: Cooperative Sequencing / Gamified Learning
Teacher Role: Display mixed email parts; prompt teams to reason about order and purpose and justify choices.
Student Role: Arrange Subject, Greeting, Opening, Body, Closing and Signature; explain each function.
AFL: Team responses, justification questions, peer correction and immediate game feedback.
3. Plan Your Email (8 mins) - Page 14
Strategy: Brainstorm-Prioritize-Pair Share
Teacher Role: Model brainstorming and grouping ideas into paragraphs; prompt campus life, feelings, plans, surprises and friends.
Student Role: Select 3-4 ideas, highlight priorities, make notes and explain the plan to a partner.
AFL: Teacher circulation, mini-conference, partner feedback and planning-note check.
4. An Email from College (11 mins) - Pages 15-17
Strategy: Guided Reading / Highlight Evidence
Teacher Role: Model locating evidence; ask main-idea/detail questions and reveal answers progressively.
Student Role: Identify email parts/main topic, match paragraph topics A-D, highlight evidence and infer vocabulary.
AFL: Cold-call questions, evidence highlighting, paragraph-topic check and progressive reveal.

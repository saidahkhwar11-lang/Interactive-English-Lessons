Lesson Title

Chapter 1 - Writing		Duration

Part 1
Key Learning Objectives• Identify the main parts and organization of a friendly email.
• Use relevant college-life vocabulary to plan an email.
• Read a model email for main idea and supporting details.
Success Criteria• I can identify the main parts of an email.
• I can plan relevant ideas before writing.
• I can identify the main idea and details in a model email.
H.O.T. QuestionWhy does organizing an email into clear paragraphs make communication more effective?
Keywords / New Vocabularyemail, subject, greeting, opening paragraph, body, closing statement, signature, dorm, campus, practice, afraid, across from

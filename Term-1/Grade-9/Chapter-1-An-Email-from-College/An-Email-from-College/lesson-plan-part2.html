Part 2
Key Learning Objectives• Use prepositions and direction language to describe locations accurately.
• Interpret campus and town maps to locate places.
• Review the organization and main parts of a friendly email.
Success Criteria• I can use location prepositions correctly.
• I can find places on a map using written clues.
• I can identify the main parts of a friendly email.
H.O.T. QuestionHow do precise prepositions help a reader understand a place without seeing it?
Keywords / New Vocabularyacross from, in, next to, on, on campus, on the corner of, direction, campus, map, greeting, paragraph, closing, signature

Teaching & Learning Procedures

Stage / TimingLearning ActivityTeacher's RoleStudents' RoleAFLDifferentiation
Starter
7 min		Campus map review: recall known direction/location words.Prompt students to describe relative positions on the map.Point, describe and recall prepositions.Cold call; pair rehearsal.H: full descriptions.
M: target phrases.
L: word bank.
Main 1
15 min		Pages 16-17 campus map: locate six campus places.Model using text clues + map; reveal answers on right panel.Read clues, locate places and record letters.Observation; progressive reveal.H: justify with evidence.
M: locate accurately.
L: one clue at a time.
Main 2
18 min		Page 18 town map: complete five sentences with location prepositions.Model first example; question relationships between places.Study map and complete sentences.Oral checks; reveal answers.H: create extra sentences.
M: complete all items.
L: use preposition bank.
Exit Ticket
5 min		Picture 15: Preparing to Write - review greeting, opening paragraph, paragraph topics, closing and signature.Ask students to identify each element and its purpose.Name each part and explain one purpose.Exit check; self-assessment.H: explain effect on reader.
M: identify all parts.
L: match labels to definitions.

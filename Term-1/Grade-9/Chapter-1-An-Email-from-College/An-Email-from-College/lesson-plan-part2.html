1. Campus Map Locations (15 mins) - Pages 16-17
Strategy: Map Reading / Evidence Hunt
Teacher Role: Model one location clue, think aloud using map landmarks, question spatial relationships and prompt evidence.
Student Role: Read each clue, locate the place, record the correct letter and justify with map/text evidence.
AFL: Targeted questioning, pair verification, evidence check and progressive answer reveal.
2. Focusing on a Map (18 mins) - Page 18
Strategy: Visual Literacy / Preposition Challenge
Teacher Role: Review the preposition bank, model one sentence from the map, then prompt students to explain why each preposition fits.
Student Role: Study the town map; complete sentences using across from, in, next to, on and on the corner of; compare and correct.
AFL: Mini-whiteboard/oral checks, partner reasoning, teacher circulation and one-by-one reveal.

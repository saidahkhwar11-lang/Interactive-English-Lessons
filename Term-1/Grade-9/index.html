📖 SOURCE: Student Book · Activity 10

✍️ Blog Instructions

  • Give your blog a clear title.
  • Start with a short opening sentence.
  • Share 2–3 things you need to do to be successful in college.
  • Give a reason or example.
  • Finish with a short closing thought.

📝 Example Blog

My Habits for College Success

I think being successful in college starts with good habits. First, I will prepare before class and complete my assignments on time. I will also take part in discussions and ask a tutor for help when I need it. These habits will help me become a more independent and successful student.

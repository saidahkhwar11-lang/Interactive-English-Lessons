Starter · Activity 1 · page 24 · open discussion

Before you read

Look at the pictures. Discuss the textbook questions with a partner; more than one opinion can be right.

1. Which kind of shopping do you prefer? Why?
2. How does online shopping make life easier?
3. What is better about visiting a real store?

Share a reason and respond respectfully to another opinion.

Activity 2 · page 24 · listen and notice

Words for the reading

First, brainstorm shopping words. Then play the audio, follow the chart, and highlight any word you do not understand. Circle words that match your brainstorm.

Which two words do you want to investigate in the reading? Tell a partner why you chose them.
Strategy · Skills Focus · page 25

Find meanings using punctuation

A word’s meaning may appear in parentheses ( ) or after a dash —. Look closely at the words beside the punctuation.

Try three new examples. Discuss before revealing each answer.

1. The store has many categories (groups of similar items).
categories = groups of similar items. The clue is inside parentheses.
2. She chose a gourmet meal — a very special, high-quality meal.
gourmet = high-quality or special. The clue follows a dash.
3. A customer (a person who buys something) wrote a review.
customer = a person who buys something. The clue is inside parentheses.
Activity 3 · page 25 · before reading

What do you predict?

Circle the option you think is correct for each textbook statement. Explain one prediction. We will check with the article after reading.

These are predictions, so keep your original choices. After reading, underline evidence before changing an answer. Paragraph A helps check statement 1.
Reading · page 25 · teacher model

Read paragraph A together

Follow the printed paragraph as one student reads with the teacher. Point to the clue for online, then say the main topic in one sentence.

Model the strategy

1. Which word has a meaning in parentheses?

2. What did only scientists and government workers know in the 1980s?

3. What is paragraph A mostly about?

online means on the Internet. The parentheses tell us directly.
Only scientists and people in the government knew about the Internet and how to use it.
Main topic: The Internet changed from something known to a few people into a big part of everyday life.
Exit Ticket · Clue Quest · 5 minutes

Choose the meaning—and the clue

Read each sentence. Pick a meaning, then tell the class whether parentheses or a dash helped you.

Exit check: “I found the meaning of ___ by looking after the parentheses / dash.”

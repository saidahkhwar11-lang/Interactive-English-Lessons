Main 1 · Prepositions of Place (10 min) · Page 18.
Strategy: Visual map reading + guided problem solving. Teacher Role: model how to locate the apartment first, think aloud through one item, question spatial relationships and reveal answers progressively. Student Role: locate places, select prepositions, explain choices and write extra sentences if finished early. AFL: targeted questioning, pair verification, answer reveal.
Main 2 · Email Language Sort (10 min) · Page 19.
Strategy: Collaborative classification. Teacher Role: clarify the four categories, circulate, challenge choices and facilitate peer checking. Student Role: sort phrases into Greetings, Opening Statements, Closing Statements and Signatures; swap/check with another pair. AFL: observation, peer assessment, category reveal.
Main 3 · Apply: Plan and Write Your Email (15 min).
Strategy: Model → Plan → Independent application. Teacher Role: revisit Sunny’s email, model a brief plan, conference with students and prompt use of Chapter 1 vocabulary/prepositions. Student Role: choose a topic, organize ideas, begin writing with correct structure, vocabulary and place language. AFL: teacher circulation, live feedback and writing evidence.
35 min
Plenary / Exit TicketPeer / Self Assessment (5 min): students use the checklist to check email format, organization, Chapter 1 vocabulary, prepositions of place, conventions and one improvement. AFL: peer feedback + reflection.5 min

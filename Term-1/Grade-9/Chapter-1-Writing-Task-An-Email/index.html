Main Writing · Plan and Begin Your Email

Suggested topics from the PPT

Your college campusYour neighborhoodA place you visitedYour new friendsYour classes

Use Chapter 1 vocabulary and at least two prepositions of place.

Greeting
Begin with an appropriate greeting: Dear… / Hi…
Opening
Open warmly and introduce why you are writing.
Body Paragraph
Write ONE focused paragraph with a clear main idea and supporting details.
Closing
Finish with an appropriate closing statement.
Signature
Add a suitable sign-off and your name.

Exit Ticket · Peer / Self Assessment

Swap work with a partner or use this as a self-check.

✓ Email structure is complete.
✓ Each paragraph has a clear idea.
✓ Chapter 1 vocabulary is used meaningfully.
✓ Location prepositions are accurate.
✓ Capitals, spelling and punctuation are checked.
✓ Give one specific helpful comment.
Reflection: What is one thing you will improve before submitting your email?

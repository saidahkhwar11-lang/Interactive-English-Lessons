Main 1 · Vocabulary & T-chart · Page 5, Activities 3–4

Classify vocabulary and compare a city with a town. AFL: Pair check and sequential reveal.

Main 2 · Language & Conversation · Page 5, Activity 5

Use There are some… / There aren’t any… and conduct a paired conversation. AFL: Speaking checklist and peer feedback.

Main 3 · Predict & Listen · Page 6, Activities 6–7

Predict four interview questions, listen twice, then answer six multiple-choice items. Teacher Role: Model, play/replay and probe evidence. Student Role: Predict, listen, choose, compare and self-correct. AFL: Prediction check, response cards and sequential answers.		30 min
MMedium: Use a word bank, question stems and partner discussion.
LLow: Use textbook visuals, categorized vocabulary, sentence frames and guided replay.

Stage 1 · Questions & Planning

Students choose Topic 1: compare city and town life and state a preference, or Topic 2: decide whether they would prefer to live on a college campus. They answer guiding questions and organize topic sentence, reasons, details and conclusion. AFL: Planning checklist + pair explanation.

Stage 2 · Research Quest

Students gather relevant ideas through discussion, books/previous reading, notebooks and laptop research. They record short notes, identify the source, and decide KEEP or SKIP. AFL: Evidence check and teacher conferencing.

Stage 3 · Extended Paragraph

Students use their plan and evidence to begin writing. They use linking words and examples and check paragraph structure. AFL: Self-check + peer check.		37 min
MMedium: Use question prompts, paragraph frame, linking-word bank and partner research.
LLow: Use visual topic choices, sentence starters, idea bank and teacher-guided planning.
الختام
Plenary

Exit Ticket · Paragraph Doctor

Students identify the missing or weak paragraph element, then check their own paragraph for the same feature. AFL: Quick diagnosis + self-correction.		3 min

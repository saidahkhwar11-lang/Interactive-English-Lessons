Main Activity 1 — Vocabulary & Listening · Page 9

Procedure: Students study the key phrases, listen to the textbook audio, and complete six sentences using the correct phrases.

Teacher’s Role: Model pronunciation, play/replay audio, monitor and elicit evidence.

Students’ Role: Listen, identify, complete, compare and self-correct.

AFL: Pair check + one-by-one answer reveal.

Main Activity 2 — Reading: What Do You Need to Do to Be Successful in College? · Pages 10–12

Procedure: Students read the continuous article, match the six successful-study habits, highlight evidence, answer three comprehension questions and complete a Deep Thinking Challenge.

Teacher’s Role: Model evidence finding, question, scaffold and provide feedback.

Students’ Role: Read, highlight, match, justify and evaluate.

AFL: Evidence highlighting, peer discussion, questioning and answer reveal.

30 min
MMedium: Use pair discussion, sentence starters and guided evidence prompts.
LLow: Use visual support, phrase bank, highlighted keywords and partner guidance.
الختام
Plenary

Exit Ticket — Vocabulary Challenge + Charter Connection

Procedure: Groups solve four vocabulary clues, reveal answers one by one, then reflect on which habit best represents lifelong learning.

Teacher’s Role: Facilitate game, check understanding and prompt reflection.

Students’ Role: Define, discuss, justify and reflect.

AFL: Exit responses and group explanation.

10 min

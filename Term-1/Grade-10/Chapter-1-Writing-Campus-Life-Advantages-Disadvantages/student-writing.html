Writing Question: What are the advantages and disadvantages of living on a college campus?

Before you write: Use your own ideas. Develop TWO advantages and TWO disadvantages. Add reasons/details, linking words and a thoughtful conclusion.

3. Disadvantages

Give a balanced final judgment. Do not simply repeat the topic sentence.

Stage 1 · Question & Planning

Students unpack the writing question: What are the advantages and disadvantages of living on a college campus? They retrieve ideas from the two reading lessons and organize a balanced planner: topic sentence, advantages, disadvantages, personal evaluation and conclusion. AFL: Plan check + partner justification.

Stage 2 · Research Evidence Hunt

Students gather short notes from discussion, previous readings/books, notebooks and laptop. They judge relevance and connect evidence to a pro or con. AFL: KEEP/SKIP challenge + evidence conference.

Stage 3 · Extended Paragraph

Teacher first displays a complete model paragraph and highlights its structure. Students then open the live Student Writing Page, write and submit independently using their own plan/evidence, while the teacher monitors the Teacher Live Board. Each submitted section has its own evaluation checks, followed by an overall writing check. AFL: live section-by-section teacher evaluation + feedback.		37 min

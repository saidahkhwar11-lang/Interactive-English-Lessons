Main Activity 1 — Six Reading Missions · Page 17

Students read paragraphs A–F one at a time. For each paragraph, they identify the main idea and answer two detail questions. Answers reveal sequentially beside their questions. Teacher models evidence-finding in Paragraph A, monitors paired reading and uses the Deep Thinking challenge for extension. AFL: Main-idea checks, text evidence, answer counter, and oral justification.

Teacher Role: Model skimming, question evidence, clarify vocabulary and guide comparison across paragraphs.

Student Role: Read, identify, locate, discuss, answer and justify.

33 min
MMedium: Use paragraph focus tags, key words and partner discussion before revealing.
LLow: Read shorter chunks, use highlighted key phrases and sentence frames: “The paragraph is mainly about…”

ACTIVITY 2 · Before Summarizing5 min

What Should We Put in Mind?

Before writing a summary, make sure you understand the paragraph.
1. Understand unfamiliar words using context clues.
2. Find the main idea.
3. Choose only the important details.
4. Use your own words — do not copy the whole paragraph.
5. Keep it short and clear.
✎ Remember: Understand → Main Idea → Key Details → Own Words → Short Summary
ACTIVITY 3 · Useful Phrases4 min

Phrases We Can Use

Use these phrases to organize your summary clearly.
Overall, ...

The paragraph mainly explains ...

In particular, ...

The key point is ...

In short, ...
ACTIVITY 4 · Model Paragraph5 min · Whole Class

Let’s Summarize Together

Read Paragraph A. Before we summarize, identify any words that may be difficult to understand.
Feeling Under the Weather? — Biometeorologists and Their Research

A   Weather has a powerful impact on the physical world. It also affects people’s personalities. How do we know about the effects of weather on people? We know from biometeorologists. These scientists study weather. They study how atmospheric conditions affect human health and emotions. The word atmosphere means “the air around the earth”. “Atmospheric conditions at a time or place” is a definition of the word weather. Some examples of these conditions are sun, wind, rain, snow, humidity (the amount of moisture in the air), and air pressure (the force of air). The weather conditions of the atmosphere greatly influence (or affect) people’s health, thinking, and feelings.

ACTIVITY 6 · Build the Summary8 min · Whole Class

Step 2 — Main Idea → Key Details → Summary

Main Idea: What is the paragraph mainly explaining?
✎ Weather and atmospheric conditions can affect people’s health, thinking and emotions.
Key Details: Which details must we keep?
✎ Biometeorologists study how weather affects people.
✎ Atmospheric conditions include humidity and air pressure.
✎ These conditions can influence health, thinking and feelings.
Now use our summary phrases and put the ideas into your own words.
✎ Overall, the paragraph explains how weather can affect people as well as the physical world. In particular, biometeorologists study how atmospheric conditions, such as humidity and air pressure, influence human health, thinking and emotions. In short, different weather conditions can have a significant effect on how people feel and function.
ACTIVITY 7 · Individual Work10 min · Individual

Open Your Book — Your Turn

Open the reading text in your book. Choose the assigned paragraph and summarize it in your notebook.
Follow the same steps:

1. Figure out unfamiliar words using context clues.
2. Write the main idea.
3. Select the key details.
4. Write a short summary in your own words.
5. Use at least one useful summarizing phrase.
✎ Context Clues → Main Idea → Key Details → Summary
ACTIVITY 8 · Exit Ticket & Share3 min

Share Your Summary

Share your summary with a partner or the class.
Exit Ticket:

1. Read your summary aloud.
2. Name one context clue that helped you understand a word.
3. Tell us the main idea of your paragraph.
✎ A good summary is short, clear, focused on the main idea and key details, and written in your own words.

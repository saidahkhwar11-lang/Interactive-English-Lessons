Procedure: Students observe the bike-sharing images and discuss the four textbook questions using Think–Pair–Share.

Teacher Role: Display the page, prompt opinions and invite evidence or examples.

Student Role: Think, discuss, listen and share one response.

AFL: Oral questioning and observation.

Main Activity 1 — Brainstorming · Page 15 · Activity 2

Pairs expand four idea boards about things people share, reasons, responsibilities and safe sharing. Teacher models one example; students contribute and categorize. AFL: Pair check and sample reveal. Time: 7 min.

Main Activity 2 — Reading and Listening · Page 15 · Activity 3

Students listen to the 57-second vocabulary track, circle items already brainstormed and check familiar items not previously listed. Teacher plays/replays audio and checks pronunciation. AFL: Interactive word-state check and oral examples. Time: 8 min.

Main Activity 3 — Plan Your Writing · Page 16 · Activity 4

Students use the six textbook planning sections to define the sharing economy, list three advantages, add examples, list three disadvantages with examples, and write a conclusion. Teacher scaffolds balanced reasoning; students plan individually then peer-check. AFL: Planning checklist, peer feedback and model reveal. Time: 18 min.

Create a NEW interactive classroom lesson webpage using the permanent design system and classroom behavior established in the approved lessons, especially the FINAL current Grade 9 “Success in College” lesson and the original “Ready to Move to College?” lesson.

IMPORTANT ORGANIZATION RULES
1. Every new lesson must have its own unique folder, webpage and published link.
2. Never overwrite, rename, replace or delete an existing lesson unless the user explicitly asks to update that exact lesson.
3. Keep lessons organized by Term → Grade → Lesson Title.
4. At the end provide the published lesson link and a complete editable backup when requested.
5. Reuse the complete working lesson architecture, not a simplified summary page.
6. The final approved Grade 9 Success in College lesson is the PRIMARY visual/layout reference. Preserve its stable working architecture; do not reintroduce experimental picture-hide scripts or other changes that previously broke activity opening, refresh, or Live Lesson Plan.

PERMANENT CLASSROOM DESIGN
1. Use the approved book-style lesson header, clean cards, rounded corners and classroom-board typography.
2. Show Grade, Teacher, lesson/unit/skill, book pages and automatic UAE day/date.
3. Keep Previous, All Activities and Next navigation.
4. Keep Start, Pause and Reset timer controls for each activity.
5. Keep Full Screen/maximize support.
6. Use the established turquoise, navy, yellow, coral, white and light-grey visual system.
7. The interface must remain clean and uncluttered. Do not leave large blank white media areas.

MAIN LESSON DASHBOARD
1. Show Objectives, Success Criteria, Key Vocabulary and Lesson Flow.
2. Show all activity cards in sequence.
3. Each card shows title, activity type, short purpose, timing and book-page metadata.
4. Normal sequence: Starter → optional Main Activities (maximum 5) → Exit Ticket → Charter Connection Activity.

PERMANENT ACTIVITY SCREEN LAYOUT
Each launched activity uses two columns.

LEFT / MAIN TEACHING AREA
1. This is the dominant/wider area (approximately 74% on normal desktop/classroom displays).
2. Put ALL questions, tasks, answer controls, revealed answers, reading paragraphs, games and Deep Thinking here.
3. Use ONE clean answer-reveal control per activity wherever several questions belong to the same exercise. Each click reveals the next answer in sequence, one by one, beside its matching question. After the final answer, the same control may switch to Hide All / Reset Answers.
4. Avoid repeating a separate Reveal Answer button under every question when a single sequential reveal button gives a cleaner classroom view.
5. Revealed answers appear directly with their question on the LEFT/main area.
6. Never put activity questions, answer controls, answers, games or Deep Thinking in the right panel.
7. For textbook exercises, rewrite all questions clearly on the main area even if a textbook screenshot is also available.
8. For reading lessons, preserve readable paragraph-by-paragraph text when useful and place comprehension questions without covering the text.
9. Exit Ticket must be a complete interactive game/activity on the main area and include Restart Game.
10. Charter Connection must be a complete attractive activity on the main area using Think → Discuss → Write → Share.

RIGHT / FROZEN STUDENT GUIDE PANEL — FINAL APPROVED DESIGN
The right side is ONLY for Key Words, Activity Objective, Student Instructions, and Lesson Steps / Agenda.
1. Keep the ENTIRE right column thin/compact (approximately 26% of the activity screen on normal desktop/classroom displays), not merely narrower text cards. Give the main teaching area the larger share of the screen.
2. Use the final soft visual style from the Grade 9 Success in College lesson: very light blue/teal guide background, white Objective card, soft pale-yellow Instructions card, white Lesson Step cards, subtle borders/shadows, rounded corners, and restrained blue/teal numbered badges.
3. Do NOT use a large dark navy guide block. Keep colors soft, bright and classroom-friendly.
4. Keep spacing compact so the guide does not dominate the board.
5. Include a small ACTIVITY GUIDE heading and a clearly separated LESSON STEPS label.
6. Key Words must be compact and easy to scan.
7. Objective must be visually distinct but not oversized.
8. Instructions must ALWAYS show the exact textbook reference at the top in a small visible badge, for example: “📖 Page 9 · Activity 1” or “📖 Page 9 · Activities 2–3”. Students should immediately know where to go in their book. For non-book activities, use a suitable label such as “Charter Connection · Class Activity”.
9. Below the page/activity badge, show only clear student-facing instructions.
10. Lesson Steps should use compact numbered cards and remain easy to scan.
11. Keep the panel frozen/sticky and independently scrollable when needed.
12. Do not put questions, answers, reveal buttons, games, Deep Thinking, teacher setup controls, or teacher-only notes in this panel.

TEACHER MEDIA WORKFLOW
1. Every activity may have optional teacher-added media.
2. Teacher can add media by drag/drop, paste with Ctrl+V, or Browse.
3. Support one or multiple images and common audio formats such as MP3, M4A and WAV.
4. Save teacher-added media automatically with IndexedDB so it remains after refresh, closing and reopening on the same browser/device.
5. Provide Replace and Delete controls in Teacher Media Setup.
6. Teacher Media Setup must be separate from student instructions and hidden in classroom/display mode.
7. Media is optional: every activity must remain fully usable without uploaded media.
8. Saved activity pictures must display as SMALL, clean thumbnail cards rather than large slides by default.
9. Each thumbnail must have a clear Maximize option and clicking the thumbnail may also maximize it.
10. Never stretch a saved activity picture across a large empty white container. Keep the default media display compact and visually neat.
11. Audio should display as a compact classroom-friendly player and must not autoplay.
12. Browser-saved media is local to that browser/device; do not imply that it transfers automatically to another computer.
13. IMPORTANT STABILITY RULE: do NOT hide the book/saved picture behind a new toggle/button or load experimental media-hiding scripts. The approved stable compact thumbnail behavior should be preserved unless the user explicitly requests a tested redesign.

BOOK PICTURES / MAXIMIZED VIEW
1. All useful textbook and teacher-added images can be maximized.
2. Keep images clear, fitted and unobstructed.
3. Preserve working highlight / clear-marks behavior where available.
4. In maximized view controls must never cover questions or content.
5. If a textbook picture is missing or unclear, the rewritten activity content must still contain everything students need.

QUESTION / ANSWER BEHAVIOR
1. Reveal answers one by one.
2. For multi-question exercises, default to ONE sequential Reveal Next Answer button for the whole activity. Each click reveals the next answer beside its own question.
3. After all answers are revealed, the same control should provide Hide All / Reset Answers, or an equally simple reset control.
4. Use separate per-question answer buttons only when the activity type genuinely requires independent question control.
5. Reveal All / Hide All may be added only when useful and must not make the view cluttered.
6. For vocabulary matching, shuffle pictures/choices so answer order is not copied from the source.
7. For suitable reading/listening tasks, include one hidden Deep Thinking Challenge connected to the real lesson idea.
8. Deep Thinking stays on the main/left teaching area, hidden by default behind a compact reveal control.

LISTENING LESSONS
1. Support teacher-added audio using the permanent saved-media workflow.
2. Include Play, Pause, Restart, progress, current/total time, volume and optional playback speed.
3. Do not autoplay.
4. Use suitable stages: prediction/vocabulary → gist → details → follow-up.
5. Transcript, when useful, is hidden by default behind Reveal Transcript.
6. Listening answers reveal one by one on the main area, preferably through the same clean sequential reveal pattern when multiple questions are grouped together.

EXIT TICKET
1. Make it a real interactive classroom game, not only plain written questions.
2. Use no more than about 5 core questions/clues unless the lesson genuinely needs more.
3. Answers reveal one by one on the main area.
4. Include Restart Game that resets the whole activity.
5. Teacher-added media is optional support only.

CHARTER CONNECTION
1. Keep it after Exit Ticket.
2. Infer the best axis from: Identity and Belonging; Citizenship and Responsibility; Thinking and Problem Solving; Lifelong Learning; Global Mindset and Tolerance.
3. Use Think 1 min → Discuss 2 min → Write → Share.
4. Make it visually attractive even with no uploaded picture.
5. Teacher may optionally add/replace a visual using Teacher Media.

LIVE BILINGUAL SCHOOL LESSON PLAN
1. Use the exact approved two-page bilingual school lesson-plan template. Never simplify or redesign it unless explicitly requested.
2. Keep the lesson plan synchronized with the actual activity content, objectives, vocabulary, timing and differentiation.
3. Include Date, Subject, Teacher, Lesson #/Title, Lesson Topic, Objectives, Success Criteria, HOT Question, Keywords, Starter, Main Activities, Differentiation H/M/L, Teacher Role, Student Role, AFL, Timing, Plenary, Learning Skills, Cross Curricular and UAE Culture/Vision 2031.
4. ALWAYS include the actual clickable “Open Interactive Lesson” link inside the lesson plan itself. This is mandatory for every lesson.
5. Include Print / Save PDF.
6. Preserve the lesson-plan styling that was approved in the current Success in College lesson.

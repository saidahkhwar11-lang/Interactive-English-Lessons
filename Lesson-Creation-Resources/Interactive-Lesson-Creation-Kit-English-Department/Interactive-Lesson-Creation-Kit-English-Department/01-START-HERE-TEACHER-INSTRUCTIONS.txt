INTERACTIVE LESSON CREATION KIT — START HERE
Al Reyadah English Department

Teachers only need the Master Prompt + Teacher Request Template.

STEP 1
Open a NEW ChatGPT chat.

STEP 2
Upload:
1. 02-MASTER-PROMPT.txt
2. Clear pictures/screenshots of the textbook pages you want to teach.
3. Audio files if it is a listening lesson.

STEP 3
Open 03-TEACHER-REQUEST-TEMPLATE.txt.
Fill in the lesson basics:
Grade, Term, Chapter/Unit, Skill, Lesson Title, Book Pages, Teacher, and 45 minutes.
Then explain which picture/page/audio belongs to:
Starter → Main Activities → Exit Ticket.

STEP 4
Paste the completed Teacher Request into ChatGPT and include:
“Create my interactive lesson using our approved department format.”

STEP 5
ChatGPT creates the interactive lesson files and approved lesson plan.
Download the lesson folder/ZIP.

STEP 6
Extract the ZIP and double-click index.html.
The lesson should open in the browser and be ready for the classroom board.

THAT'S IT.
No GitHub. No publishing. No coding.

BEFORE YOU START
- Use clear textbook pictures.
- Upload audio as MP3/M4A/WAV when possible.
- Keep files in lesson order so ChatGPT can understand them.
- If one textbook activity uses several screenshots, say which screenshots belong together.
- Do not ask ChatGPT to invent missing textbook content.

BEFORE CLASS
Quickly check Previous/Next, timers, fullscreen, answer reveals, audio (if used), Exit Ticket restart, and lesson-plan link.

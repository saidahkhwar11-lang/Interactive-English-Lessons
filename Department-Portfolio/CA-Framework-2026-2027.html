English Department Continuous Assessment (CA) Framework

Al Reyadah School · Academic Year 2026–2027

Purpose: The Continuous Assessment framework provides a consistent department-wide structure for monitoring students’ performance across the main English language skills. CA is calculated out of 100% and reflects ongoing performance throughout the term rather than one single assessment.
Assessment AreaWeightingWhat It IncludesWhat It Measures
Spelling25%Spelling tests; high-frequency and topic-related words; spelling activities.Accurate spelling; application of spelling rules; accuracy and confidence in written English.
Reading25%Reading comprehension tasks; varied text types; vocabulary and inference questions.Understanding texts and main ideas; locating information; vocabulary, inference and critical thinking.
Writing15%Writing for different purposes; organisation of ideas; vocabulary, grammar and punctuation.Clear and effective communication; appropriate language use; organisation and creativity.
Speaking10%Class discussions; presentations and speaking tasks; oral communication activities.Clear expression; appropriate vocabulary and pronunciation; interaction and response.
Listening10%Spoken texts, conversations and instructions; comprehension questions; identifying information.Main ideas and key details; following instructions; active listening skills.
Coursework15%ALEF tasks and activities; notebook work; class participation and activities.Responsibility and organisation; active participation; consistent effort and completion of assigned work.
Department note: Each component contributes to a comprehensive picture of student attainment and progress. Assessment evidence should be used to identify strengths, learning gaps, appropriate support, challenge and next steps.
